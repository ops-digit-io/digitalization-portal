## Problem

Maintenance shift handovers happen on paper and verbally. Open issues get lost between shifts.

## Current pain

Each handover takes 20–30 minutes and things still fall through. Repeat breakdowns because the incoming shift didn't know a fix was half-done.

## Desired outcome

A structured digital handover that carries open issues, part status and half-done work across shifts.

## Affected process

Maintenance shift change, three shifts a day.

## Frequency & scale

3 handovers/day, ~15 maintenance staff per plant.
