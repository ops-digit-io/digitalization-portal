## Problem

Preparing supplier tenders means re-reading dozens of past tenders and specs by hand.

## Current pain

A tender takes a buyer 2–3 days to assemble. Much of it is copy-paste from prior tenders that are hard to find.

## Desired outcome

An assistant that drafts a tender from the request and past comparable tenders, leaving the buyer to review and decide.

## Affected process

Procurement, tender preparation. Category buyers.

## Frequency & scale

~8 tenders a month across the category team.

## Constraints & context

Past tenders live in a shared drive with inconsistent naming.
