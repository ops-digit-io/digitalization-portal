## Problem

> Original (DE): Die Schichtübergabe läuft über einen Zettel, der oft verloren geht.

Shift handover is a paper note that is frequently incomplete or lost, so the incoming shift starts blind on open issues.

## Current pain

Recurring re-work and missed follow-ups; no measurement today, but every shift lead reports it (requester estimate).

## Desired outcome

A structured digital handover that carries open issues, safety notes, and follow-ups to the next shift and is searchable later.

## Proposed solution

A simple structured handover form on the line terminal, feeding a searchable log.

## Classification rationale

L1 — the handover workflow changes shape. Heat medium — broad quality-of-life and traceability gain, low tech risk.
