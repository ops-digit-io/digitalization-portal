# Requirements · UC-2026-0041

## Epics

### E1 · Shift-resolved scrap attribution
- **US-1** As a shift lead, I see scrap for my shift by cause code by the next morning, so I can act while the batch is fresh.
- **US-2** As a quality engineer, I see a shift-resolved Pareto of cause codes over a selectable window.

## Non-functional
- **NFR-1 (freshness)** Attribution is available within 12h of the scrap posting.
- **NFR-2 (traceability)** Every attributed record links back to its MES posting id.
