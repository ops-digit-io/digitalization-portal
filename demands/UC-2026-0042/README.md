## Problem

> Original (EN): Preparing a tender response takes two senior buyers a full week each time.

Assembling a tender response means manually gathering prior responses, spec sheets, and pricing from scattered folders — a week of senior-buyer time per tender.

## Current pain

~40 tenders/year × ~1 buyer-week each; senior-buyer time is the bottleneck (procurement estimate).

## Desired outcome

A buyer drafts a tender response in a day by retrieving and assembling prior approved content, with every figure traceable to its source.

## Proposed solution

A retrieval assistant over the approved tender/spec corpus that drafts sections a buyer edits — never auto-submits.

## Classification rationale

L1 — the workflow changes shape (assemble-then-edit vs. gather-from-scratch). Heat high — large time saving, corpus already exists.
