# Requirements · UC-2026-0042

## Epics

### E1 · Retrieval-assisted tender drafting
- **US-1** As a buyer, I retrieve the most relevant approved passages for a tender section and get an editable draft.
- **US-2** As a buyer, every drafted claim shows its source document so I can verify before sending.

## Non-functional
- **NFR-1 (grounding)** No drafted figure without a cited source passage.
- **NFR-2 (confidentiality)** The corpus never leaves the EU tenancy.
