## Scope

**In scope.** Draft assistance for tender narrative and spec sections from the approved corpus.

**Out of scope.** Price optimization; auto-submission; supplier-facing chat.

**Global potential.** Global — procurement operates one tender process across sites.

## Baseline

**Metric.** Senior-buyer hours per tender response.

**Value.** ~40 buyer-hours per tender × 40 tenders/year (procurement time study).

**Method.** Time study of 8 recent tenders vs. assisted drafts in the pilot.

**Source.** Procurement time tracking.

**Owner.** proc.controlling@example.com

**Verified.** No

## Value

**Category.** productivity
**Annual gross.** €240,000
**Basis.** Halving ~1,600 senior-buyer hours/year at a loaded €300/h equivalent.
