# network-coverage

Read the champion register against the organisation it is supposed to cover, and
say where the network cannot carry work. The finding is always about the NETWORK.

## The four shapes of a hole

Look for these in order. They are ordered by how completely they stop work, not
by how many cells they affect.

1. **Uncovered.** A plant × domain cell with nobody at all. Nothing can be raised
   here, so the absence of demand from this area is not a signal about the area —
   it is a signal about the register. Say that explicitly, because an uncovered
   plant looks exactly like a contented one in every other view in the portal.
2. **No decider.** A cell with a champion but no spoke. Work can be carried and
   nothing can be approved; this is the most common way an engagement stalls
   after starting well, and it is invisible until someone tries to change
   something.
3. **Single point.** One person covering a whole plant, or the only person in a
   domain across every site. Not a criticism of them — a structural risk that
   belongs to the hub. Name the cells that go dark if they step back.
4. **Hub carrying local work.** A hub-role person named as the owner or champion
   of engagements at a specific site. Each one is the operating model failing in
   miniature: the centre doing what the network was meant to do.

## What to do with each

Every hole becomes an ACTION with a named target, not an observation. "DE-ALD
quality has nobody" is the finding; "ask the DE-ALD quality lead to name a spoke —
two engagements there are waiting on an approver" is the action. An action states
who to approach, what to ask for, and what is blocked until they answer.

Where the portal already shows someone doing the work — owning a process, being
named as a champion, raising demands — propose THEM. The strongest candidate is
almost always somebody already doing the job without the title.

## What you may not do

Do not rank people, order them by load, or describe anyone as more or less
committed. Load tells you who to relieve, never who to praise or blame. A person
carrying a great deal is a finding about how much the network asks of one node.
