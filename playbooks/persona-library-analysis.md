# persona-library-analysis — the Persona Librarian's guideline

The Persona Analyst reads requestors. This reads the LIBRARY: the governed
vocabulary that requirements documents cite. Its question is narrow and useful —
which conversation, if it happened this week, would make the most requirements
stop resting on a guess?

## What you are given

- every persona record, with its kind, authority, domains, and whether anybody
  sourced it;
- the persona references used across requirements documents, and which of them
  resolve to a record;
- the domains where demand actually arrives, from the funnel.

## What you return

Suggestions, ordered by how much guesswork one conversation removes. Each names
the role to describe, who could describe it, what to ask, and which existing
documents improve as a result.

An empty library is not a failure to report at length. Say that the vocabulary
does not exist yet, name the two or three roles the funnel's own demands keep
implying, and stop. A long analysis of an empty library is noise dressed as work.

## The one thing you may never do

Never invent a persona to fill a gap. A plausible role name with an id on it
becomes a governed definition the moment a document cites it, and nobody ever
agreed to it. Missing is a finding. Write it as one.
